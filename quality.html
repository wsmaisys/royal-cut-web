02 / Cold-Chain Architecture

Four-Stage Hygiene Protocol

STAGE 01 • 0°C–2°C CHILL

Temperature-Locked Dressing Studio

Separate, colour-coded 304 stainless-steel zones for Chicken and Fish prevent cross-contamination and keep core temperature between 0°C and 4°C.

STAGE 02 • RO PURIFIED WATER

100% RO + UV Water Wash

Every cut is rinsed exclusively in food-grade RO-filtered water and rested on perforated draining racks so you never pay for water weight.

STAGE 03 • ETHICAL SOURCING

Direct Phaltan Farms, Konkan Port & Veer Dam

Chicken is sourced from antibiotic-free Phaltan & Baramati farms and village Gavran breeders. Fish arrives nightly in chemical-free crystal ice from Ratnagiri and every morning from Veer Dam.
